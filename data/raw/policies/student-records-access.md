# Student Records Access and Use

## Scope

This policy applies to faculty, staff, and contracted systems that store or display student education records as defined by institutional policy and applicable law.

## Authorized uses

Records may be accessed for legitimate educational interests: advising, course administration, accreditation reporting, and approved research with de-identification where required.

## Minimum necessary standard

Users must request only fields required for the task. Bulk exports require approval from the Registrar and an auditable justification.

## Retention

Advising notes in official systems are retained for seven years unless superseded by Records Management schedule updates.

## Incident reporting

Suspected unauthorized access must be reported to the Information Security office within one business day.
