# Selected Publication Abstracts — Dr. L. Chen

## Efficient indexing for campus knowledge graphs

We present a disk-friendly adjacency layout that reduces random I/O for department-scale knowledge graphs while preserving predictable rebuild times for nightly ETL jobs.

## Auditing retrieval pipelines in regulated environments

This work defines provenance records required to reconstruct citation paths in university policy assistants and evaluates audit completeness under partial document updates.

## Teaching note: reproducible benchmarking

We describe a minimal harness for comparing chunking policies on fixed corpora without coupling benchmarks to a specific embedding model release.
