# Undergraduate Computer Science Programs

## Program overview

The Department of Computer Science offers B.S. and B.A. tracks. Both require core coursework in programming, discrete structures, systems, and theory before upper-division electives.

## Degree requirements

Students must complete 120 credits, including 42 credits in major requirements. A capstone project or approved research sequence is required in the senior year.

## Advising

Declared majors are assigned a faculty advisor by concentration. Students on academic probation must meet an advisor each term before registration.

## Course numbering

Courses numbered 100–199 are introductory, 200–299 intermediate, and 300–499 advanced undergraduate work.
